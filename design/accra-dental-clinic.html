Treatments

An index, not a price list.

Every plan is quoted after a full assessment, in writing, with the alternatives and what each one costs you in maintenance. Six areas of practice — open a row to read what is actually involved.

Most dental problems begin as gum inflammation and are treated as an inconvenience rather than a disease. We chart the pocket depths, photograph the same sites at every visit, and treat inflammation before proposing anything cosmetic.

A maintenance programme runs on a three- to six-month rhythm depending on what we find. Scaling and root planing is done here, not referred out.

Charting Scaling & root planing Hygiene programme Oral cancer screening

When a tooth needs structural repair, the question is how much sound tooth we can keep. Large direct and indirect restorations are built in layers and cured properly rather than filled in one pass and hope.

Where a cusp is compromised we use an onlay rather than crowning the whole tooth. It is slower and it costs less tooth to remove.

Onlays & inlays Crowns Endodontics Implants

Nothing is prepared before you have seen a physical mock-up of the proposed change and held it against your own face. This is the step most practices skip and the reason patients are surprised by their own reflection.

Where the change can be achieved without removing enamel we say so. Additive composite first, ceramic only where additive work will not hold.

Diagnostic mock-up Composite artistry Porcelain veneers Smile design

An intraoral scan replaces the tray impression. It is more comfortable, considerably more accurate, and the file is yours — you get it on a drive, and any future dentist can work from it.

Planning happens on the scan before the appointment, which shortens time in the chair and removes the need to stop and re-measure mid-procedure.

Intraoral scanning Digital smile design Guided surgery planning

Worn facets, morning headaches and a jaw that aches by evening are not a filling problem. We take a history, screen for sleep apnoea where indicated, and treat the cause rather than resurfacing the damage every two years.

Night guards and splints are fitted from the digital record so they actually seat. Where the cause is airway-related we refer on rather than treat around it.

Bruxism Snore & apnoea screening Occlusal splints

A first appointment is a ride in the chair, a tooth count and a sticker. No instruments, no probe in the mouth, nothing that needs numbing. It buys us six years of patients who are not frightened of the room.

Fissure sealants and fluoride varnish are the interventions with the best evidence, and both are quick.

First visits Sealants Fluoride varnish

Approach

Three rules we
do not break.

These are not values on a wall. They are the reason the practice is one clinician and why we turn down work we should not be taking.

01

Enamel is not renewable.

Every preparation is irreversible, so we are conservative by default. If a change can be made additively, we make it additively — even when the crown route is simpler and better paid.

02

The mock-up comes first.

No lab work is commissioned before you have held a physical mock-up and looked at it in daylight. If it is wrong at that stage, it costs us a morning. If it is wrong after preparation, it costs you a tooth.

03

You leave with a record.

Digital photographs and scans go home with you. Any dentist in Accra can continue from those files. We are not asking to be the only place your teeth are known.
